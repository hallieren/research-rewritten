# Failure-mode census

**How to use.** Run a thirty-minute census (illustrative) on one project. Start from the quick reference and translate each general signature into a concrete signal in this project; a copied signature equals a blank. Fill the three steps where AI is deepest in completely, the rest in short. The cross-cutting column has no blanks (rule): motivated collusion is the conclusion you are most excited about being the one you checked least, and no self-check catches it. Refill at every milestone (before plan sign-off, after first results, before delivery) and keep the old sheets.

> The order of use is fixed. templates/failure-mode-census.md → templates/verification-budget-sheet.md → templates/verification-workflow-card.md → templates/downgrade-note.md → templates/honest-map.md.

| Failure mode | Chief attribute | High-incidence step | General signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hallucination and fabricated citations | fluency x corpus prior | Master the field, deliver | The most on-point citation is the most suspect; the retelling carries fewer qualifiers than the original |
| Spurious significance and the criteria backdoor | sycophancy x fluency | Test plan, interpretation | The criterion appears after the result; the conclusion just happens to clear the line |
| Data leakage and contamination | corpus prior | Execution | The score is too good to be true; it collapses on fresh questions from the same distribution |
| Sycophancy drift | sycophancy | Interpretation, red team | Ask twice with opposite leans and the conclusion flips |

```text
# Failure-mode census

Per step (one block each for steps 1 to 7; complete for the three steps where AI is deepest in)
Step ____: AI involvement (none / light / deep): ____   Attribute mainly consumed: ____
  Error type most likely to grow here: ____________
  Concrete signature in this project (a signal: "anything above ____ points is treated as leakage first"; "which criterion wording can still be explained away"; "which numbers changed hands more than once between analysis and draft"): ____________
  Cost of being wrong (high / medium / low; at most two highs): ____

Cross-cutting column, motivated collusion (mandatory)
  The one conclusion I am most excited about: ____________
  Which step and cell it sits in: ____________
  My actual checking intensity on it (honestly): ____________
  A channel that does not share my motive (a person's name / how an independent agent is dispatched, with which brief): ____________

The two circled cells
  Operating room (where being wrong costs most): ____________
  High-incidence zone (the cell holding the conclusion I am most excited about): ____________

Closing self-test (no answer means the census fails)
  If this project blows up in three months, most likely in cell: ____   The crime scene looks like: ____________
Signature: ____________ (must be a human)
```

### Self-check
- [ ] Concrete signature copied from the quick reference? Copying equals blank. Turn "too good to be true" into a number in this project.
- [ ] Motivated collusion column blank or filled with something harmless? You walked around the most valuable field. Name the conclusion you want.
- [ ] Operating room and high-incidence zone the same cell? That is the most dangerous position right now. Send the whole verification budget there first.
- [ ] Every cell "medium"? No ranking means no layering. Force a top two.
- [ ] Cannot answer the closing self-test? Go back to the deepest-AI step and walk the two questions again.
