# Paper card

**How to use.** One card per load-bearing paper: each camp's representative works, the repeatedly cited sources, the empirical studies tied to your question; five to fifteen cards (illustrative). The model fills the first four fields from the paper (the paper card prompt in briefs/controversy-map.md). The last field stays blank until you have read the key passages of the original; a filled "how much I believe it" is your judgment, a summary is a compression of somebody else's.

```text
# Paper card
Title: ____________   Authors: ____________   Year: ____   Venue: ____________
Exists: confirmed (tool: ________) / unverified
Camp on the controversy map: ____________   Map row(s) it bears on: ____

Core claim (one sentence, in the paper's own wording): ____________
Evidence (what experiment / data / task, at what scale): ____________
Where the claim applies (limits the authors admit, limitations section and footnotes): ____________
Which prior work it refutes or depends on: ____________

Read in the original (tick only what you opened yourself):
☐ abstract   ☐ the results table the claim rests on   ☐ limitations section   ☐ the passage I will quote
Qualifiers in the original that the summary dropped: ____________

How much I believe it (hand-filled, after reading): ____________
Signature: ____________ (must be a human)
```

### Self-check
- [ ] The belief field filled and no "read in the original" box ticked? That is the model's belief, not yours. Open the paper, then fill it.
- [ ] Core claim paraphrased instead of quoted? Paraphrase drops qualifiers. Paste the paper's wording.
- [ ] `Exists: unverified` on a card entering a decision? Search it now or the card does not enter.
- [ ] "Where the claim applies" empty? Every paper admits limits somewhere. Find the footnote.
