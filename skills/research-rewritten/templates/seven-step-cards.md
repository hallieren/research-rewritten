# Seven-step workflow check cards

**How to use.** One card per step, seven cards. Fill all seven at project start; refill the matching card when you get stuck or sent back. A blank you cannot fill is the diagnosis: it says where you are really stuck. The card locates, it does not instruct. Read the "downstream" line across all seven cards in a row; where the chain breaks is the project's real bottleneck.

| Step | Goal of the step | Example done criterion | Example send-back signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Master the field | Turn "can't read it all" into "can ask it something"; know who claims what and which argument the question lands in | I can predict what new evidence would change my map | At delivery there is a paragraph I cannot write clearly |
| 2 Questions and hypotheses | Grind a blur of curiosity into a question whose answer could embarrass me | I can say which observation would kill this hypothesis | The red team says the question itself is asked wrong |
| 3 Test plan | Lock plan, method, and above all what counts as losing, before running | Criteria signed before anything ran, not changed afterward | During interpretation the criteria did not block an illusion |
| 4 Execution | Turn the design into data inside a harness that leaves errors nowhere to hide | Tests green and results reproducible from raw data in one command | A "finding" turns out to be a bug |
| 5 Read and catch errors | Sort results into findings, noise, and bugs; the most expensive error looks like the most exciting finding | Every main conclusion checked against the standing list of illusions | The red team finds an alternative explanation I did not check |
| 6 Deliver | Turn "I know" into "others can trust"; right vehicle, every number traceable | Any number challenged gets its source within three minutes (illustrative) | I cannot answer the reader's first question |
| 7 Red team | Let the harshest criticism happen at home first | The strongest objection is written into the deliverable, not deleted | Write down the step you are most likely to be sent back to |

```text
# Step card   Step: ____ (1 to 7)   Project: ____________   Date: ____________
What I need to produce: ____________
Who is downstream (who takes it as input; for step 6, what decision they make with it): ____________
Drafter (me / AI): ______   Review mode (line by line / spot check / against locked criteria): ______
Decider: ______   Who answers when it's wrong: ______ (a person's name, or NOBODY)
The criterion for this step being done (locked before starting): ____________
The signal that sends me back to this step: ____________
For card 7 only, the step the red team is most likely to send me back to: ____
```

### Self-check
- [ ] Card 3 empty? The step most often skipped; criteria added afterward always happen to support the plan finished first. Fill it before anything runs.
- [ ] Card 7 empty? Without an internal red team the first red team is the real world. Fill it now.
- [ ] Any card where drafter, reviewer, decider, and answerer all say AI? Downgrade it against templates/ladder-self-rating.md.
- [ ] A done criterion written after the step started? It does not count. Write the next one before the next step.
